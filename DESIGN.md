# DESIGN.md

## Brand
Mavora — AI second-memory companion for people living with dementia and their families.
Audience: Patients needing calm voice and memory reassurance; family caregivers needing schedule and safety visibility.
Tone: Calm, precise, warm, editorial, respectful.

## Color tokens
--bg: #0b0b0c
--surface: #141415
--border: rgba(244, 244, 242, 0.10)
--text: #f4f4f2
--muted: #8d8d92
--accent: #9b8afb

## Typography
Display: Georgia, "Instrument Serif", serif
Body: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, system-ui, sans-serif
Mono: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, monospace
Scale: 56px (H1) / 36px (H2) / 24px (H3) / 16px (Body) / 13px (Small) / 11px (Eyebrow)
Line-height: 1.05-1.15 on headings, 1.55 on body.
Tracking: -0.025em on large headings, 0.08em on mono eyebrows.

## Spacing and shape
Spacing: 8px scale.
Section padding: 104px desktop / 64px mobile.
Max content width: 1160px.
Radius: 8px (rounded-lg).
Borders: 1px at 10% opacity (rgba(244, 244, 242, 0.10)).
Shadows: none.

## Components
Button primary: solid #f4f4f2, text #0b0b0c, hover #e2e2e0, 8px radius, no wrap.
Button secondary: 1px border at 20% opacity, transparent background, hover bg-white/5.
Card / Ruled grid: flat #0b0b0c / #141415, 1px border at 10%, no shadow, no glass blur.
Eyebrow: 11px mono uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em, text #8d8d92, no pill wrappers.

## Motion
150-200ms ease-out transitions.
3D brain: monochrome & accent (#9b8afb) only, 40s/rev rotation, pause off-screen, prefers-reduced-motion respected.

## Do not
No gradients, no glow lights or blobs, no glass cards, no neon box shadows, no emoji as icons, no stock photos, no giant eyebrows.
